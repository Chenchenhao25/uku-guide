Find instant answers to the questions our users ask most. If you don't find what you're looking for here, visit our [Common Issues](/support/common-issues) page or [Contact Support](/support/contact).

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I change the app language?">
    Go to **Settings → Language** and select your preferred language from the list. The app will update immediately without requiring a restart.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is Uku free?">
    Yes — Uku offers a free plan that you can use indefinitely. Paid plans (Pro and Premium) unlock additional features such as advanced analytics, priority support, and extended storage. Visit **Settings → Subscription** to compare plans.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I reset my password?">
    On the login screen, tap **Forgot Password** and enter the email address associated with your account. You'll receive a password reset link within a few minutes. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive in your inbox.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use Uku on multiple devices?">
    Yes. Sign in with your account credentials on any supported device and your data will sync automatically. There is no limit to the number of devices you can use simultaneously.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I cancel my subscription?">
    Navigate to **Settings → Subscription → Cancel Plan** and follow the on-screen prompts. Your access to paid features will continue until the end of your current billing period. Cancellation does not delete your account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What languages does Uku support?">
    Uku currently supports **English, Italian, French, Spanish, German, and Portuguese**. We're actively working to add more languages — check back for updates or follow our release notes for announcements.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I download my data?">
    Go to **Settings → Privacy → Download My Data**. Uku will prepare a file containing your account information and content, then send a download link to your registered email address within 24 hours.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use Uku offline?">
    Some features are available offline, allowing you to continue working without an internet connection. Any changes you make offline are saved locally and synced automatically the next time your device reconnects.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I delete my account?">
    To permanently delete your account, contact our support team at [support@uku.app](mailto:support@uku.app) and request account deletion. Please be aware that **deletion is permanent** — all your data will be removed and cannot be recovered.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why am I not receiving notifications?">
    First, check that Uku has permission to send notifications on your device:
    - **iOS:** Go to your device **Settings → Uku → Notifications** and ensure notifications are enabled.
    - **Android:** Go to **Settings → Apps → Uku → Notifications** and turn them on.

    Also open Uku and check **Settings → Notifications** to confirm your in-app notification preferences are configured correctly.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
