Most issues with Uku can be resolved quickly with a few troubleshooting steps. This page covers the most frequently reported problems and how to fix them.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can't log in to my account">
    If you're unable to sign in, work through the following steps:

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Check your credentials">
        Confirm you're using the correct email address and password. Check that **Caps Lock** is off — passwords are case-sensitive.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Reset your password">
        Tap **Forgot Password** on the login screen, enter your email, and follow the link sent to your inbox. Check your spam folder if the email doesn't arrive.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Try a different network">
        Firewall or network restrictions can sometimes block login requests. Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data, or try a different Wi-Fi network.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Contact support">
        If you still can't log in, reach out at [support@uku.app](mailto:support@uku.app) with your account email address and a description of the issue.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="App is slow or freezing">
    Performance issues are usually caused by resource constraints or an outdated version of the app. Try these steps in order:

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Close and reopen the app">
        Fully close Uku (swipe it away from your recent apps), then relaunch it.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Check for updates">
        Visit the App Store or Google Play Store and install any available updates for Uku. Updates often include performance improvements and bug fixes.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Restart your device">
        A fresh restart clears memory and background processes that may be affecting performance.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Free up storage">
        Ensure your device has sufficient free storage space. A device with less than 10% free storage can cause apps to slow down significantly.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Data is not syncing">
    If your data isn't syncing across devices, follow these steps:

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Check your internet connection">
        Make sure your device has an active and stable internet connection. Try loading a webpage to confirm.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Sign out and sign back in">
        Go to **Settings → Account → Sign Out**, then sign in again. This refreshes your session and often restores sync.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Review your sync settings">
        Open **Settings → Sync** and confirm that syncing is enabled. If you're on a limited data plan, you may have disabled background sync.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Notifications are not arriving">
    If Uku isn't sending you notifications, check permissions at both the device level and inside the app:

    - **iOS:** Go to **Settings → Uku → Notifications** on your device and make sure notifications are enabled.
    - **Android:** Go to **Settings → Apps → Uku → Notifications** and turn on all relevant notification types.
    - **Inside Uku:** Open **Settings → Notifications** and confirm that the notification types you want to receive are turned on.

    <Info>
      If you recently reinstalled Uku, your device may have reset notification permissions. You'll need to re-enable them manually using the steps above.
    </Info>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Language changed unexpectedly">
    By default, Uku follows your device's locale setting and may switch languages if you change your device language. To set a fixed language independent of your device:

    1. Open Uku and go to **Settings → Language**.
    2. Select your preferred language from the list.
    3. Tap **Save** to apply the change.

    This override will persist even if you later change your device's system language.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't complete a payment">
    If your payment is being declined or not processing, try the following:

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Check your card details">
        Re-enter your card number, expiry date, and CVV carefully to rule out any typos.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Try a different payment method">
        Attempt the payment using a different credit or debit card, or another available payment option.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Contact your bank">
        Some banks block unfamiliar or international transactions as a security measure. Call your bank or check their app to see if the payment was declined at their end.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Contact Uku support">
        If the issue persists, reach out to our team at [support@uku.app](mailto:support@uku.app) with your account email and a description of the error you're seeing.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  If none of these steps resolve your issue, please reach out on the [Contact Support](/support/contact) page. Our team is happy to help.
</Note>
