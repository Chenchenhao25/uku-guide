The Uku support team is here to help. Whether you have a question, encounter a bug, or need help with your account, here's how to reach us.

## Ways to Get Help

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Email Support" icon="envelope" href="mailto:support@uku.app">
    Send a message directly to [support@uku.app](mailto:support@uku.app). Our team responds to all email enquiries within 24 hours on business days.
  </Card>
  <Card title="In-App Chat" icon="comments" href="/support/faq">
    Tap **Help** in the app's **Settings** menu to start a live chat session with a support agent. Live chat is available during business hours.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Help Center" icon="circle-question" href="/support/faq">
    Search for answers in our [FAQ](/support/faq) and [Common Issues](/support/common-issues) guides — many questions can be resolved without waiting for a reply.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Before You Write to Us

Save time by checking these resources first — your answer may already be there:

- Have you checked the [FAQ](/support/faq)?
- Have you tried the steps in [Common Issues](/support/common-issues)?
- Can you reproduce the issue? If so, note the exact steps that trigger it.

## What to Include in Your Message

To help us resolve your issue as quickly as possible, please include the following details when you contact us:

- Your **account email address**
- Your **device type** (e.g. iPhone, Android phone, or browser)
- Your **app version** (found in **Settings → About**)
- A clear **description of the issue**, including any error messages you see
- **Screenshots or screen recordings** if possible

## Response Times

Response times vary depending on your subscription plan:

| Plan    | First Response Time |
|---------|---------------------|
| Free    | Within 72 hours     |
| Pro     | Within 24 hours     |
| Premium | Within 4 hours      |

<Tip>
  Including screenshots or a short screen recording with your message can significantly speed up resolution. A visual of the issue helps our team diagnose the problem without needing to ask follow-up questions.
</Tip>
