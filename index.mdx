## Quick Start

Get up and running with Uku in just a few steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download Uku">
    Install the Uku app on your device from the App Store or Google Play. See the [Download](/getting-started/download) page for direct links and system requirements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Sign up with your email address or continue with a social login. Learn more on the [Account Setup](/getting-started/account-setup) page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore the Features">
    Once you're in, discover what Uku can do for you. The [Features Overview](/features/overview) is the best place to start.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize Your Experience">
    Adjust your language, notification preferences, and profile settings to make Uku truly yours.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  This guide is available in multiple languages. Use the language selector in the top navigation to switch to your preferred language.
</Note>
