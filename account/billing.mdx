Uku offers a free plan along with paid options for users who want access to advanced features. Whether you're just getting started or need the full power of Uku for personal or professional use, this page covers everything related to plans and billing.

## Available Plans

Choose the plan that best fits your needs. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time from your subscription settings.

| Plan    | Price          | Key Features                                                      |
|---------|----------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Free    | $0/month       | Core features, limited storage                                    |
| Pro     | $5.99/month    | All features, 10 GB storage, priority support                     |
| Premium | $12.99/month   | All Pro features + unlimited storage, advanced analytics          |

## Upgrading Your Plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Subscription settings">
    Go to **Settings → Subscription**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the upgrade">
    Tap **Upgrade Plan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Select the plan you want to switch to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter payment details">
    Provide your credit or debit card information in the payment form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your upgrade">
    Tap **Confirm**. Your new plan is activated immediately and you gain access to all its features right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing Payment Methods

You can add, remove, or update your payment information at any time. Go to **Settings → Billing → Payment Methods** to manage your cards. To add a new card, tap **Add Payment Method** and enter your card details. To remove an existing card, select it from the list and tap **Remove**. To update card details (such as an expiry date), remove the existing card and add the updated one.

## Cancelling Your Subscription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Subscription settings">
    Go to **Settings → Subscription**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancel your plan">
    Tap **Cancel Plan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm cancellation">
    Review the cancellation details and confirm your decision.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Your cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period — you keep full access to your paid plan until that date. After cancellation, your account moves to the free plan and your data is retained.
</Note>

## Refund Policy

If you believe you were charged in error or change your mind shortly after a payment, you can request a refund within **7 days** of the charge. Refund requests made after 7 days are considered on a case-by-case basis. To submit a refund request, contact the support team at [/support/contact](/support/contact).
