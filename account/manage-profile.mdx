Your Uku profile is how the app identifies you. Keeping it up to date ensures a smooth experience across all your devices.

## Editing Your Profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    Tap your avatar in the top-right corner of the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter edit mode">
    Select **Edit Profile** from the menu that appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your details">
    Update your name, bio, or profile photo as needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Tap **Save Changes** to apply your updates.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Keep your profile photo friendly and recognizable — especially if you use any of Uku's shared or collaborative features. A clear photo helps teammates and collaborators identify you at a glance.
</Tip>

## Changing Your Email Address

To change the email address associated with your account, go to **Settings → Account** and update the email field. For security purposes, Uku will send a confirmation link to your new address. You must click that link to verify the change before it takes effect. Until confirmed, your original email address remains active.

## Changing Your Password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Security settings">
    Go to **Settings → Security**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the password change">
    Tap **Change Password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your current password">
    Type in your existing password to verify your identity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your new password">
    Enter your new password, then type it again in the confirmation field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the new password">
    Tap **Update Password**. Your new password is active immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Deleting Your Account

<Warning>
  Account deletion is permanent. Once your account is deleted, all of your data — including your profile, history, and saved content — will be lost and cannot be recovered. This action cannot be undone.
</Warning>

If you still wish to delete your account, please reach out to our support team at [/support/contact](/support/contact) and they will guide you through the process.
