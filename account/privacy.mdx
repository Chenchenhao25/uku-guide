Uku takes your privacy seriously. This page explains your privacy controls, what data is collected, and how you can manage or export it. You are always in control of the information you share with Uku.

## Your Privacy Settings

You can manage the following privacy controls from **Settings → Privacy**:

- **Profile visibility** — Choose whether your profile is **public** (visible to all Uku users) or **private** (visible only to people you approve). Switching to private limits who can find and view your profile.
- **Activity tracking** — Toggle activity tracking **on** or **off**. When off, Uku will not record your in-app activity history.
- **Data sharing for analytics** — Uku may use anonymised usage data to improve the app. You can **opt out** of this at any time without affecting your core app experience.

## Downloading Your Data

You can request a copy of all the personal data Uku holds for your account at any time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Privacy settings">
    Go to **Settings → Privacy**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Initiate a data download">
    Tap **Download My Data**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select data types">
    Choose the categories of data you want to include in your export (e.g. profile information, activity history, content).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request the download">
    Tap **Request Download**. You will receive an email with a secure download link within **24 hours**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Opting Out of Analytics

Uku collects anonymised usage analytics to understand how people use the app and to guide improvements. If you prefer not to contribute this data, you can disable it at any time. Go to **Settings → Privacy → Data & Analytics** and toggle off **Usage Analytics**. This setting takes effect immediately and does not impact any app functionality.

## Data Retention

Uku retains your account data for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, your personal data is permanently removed from Uku's systems within **30 days** of the deletion request. During that window, your data is no longer accessible through the app.

<Note>
  For full details on how Uku collects, uses, and protects your data, read the [Uku Privacy Policy](https://uku.app/privacy).
</Note>
