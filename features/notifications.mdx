Uku can keep you informed with push notifications, email alerts, and in-app messages. You have full control over what you receive and when — so you can stay up to date without feeling overwhelmed. Whether you want all notifications on, only the essentials, or complete silence, Uku makes it easy to set things up exactly the way you want.

## Types of Notifications

Uku sends several categories of notifications across different channels. Here's a breakdown of what's available and what's enabled by default:

| Type | Channel | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Activity updates | Push + In-app | On |
| Weekly summary | Email | On |
| Special offers | Email | Off |
| System alerts | Push + In-app | On |

## Managing Notifications

You can turn any notification category on or off in just a few steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings → Notifications">
    Tap your profile avatar (top-right), select **Settings**, then tap **Notifications** to open the notifications preferences screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle notification categories">
    Use the toggle switches next to each category to turn them on or off. Changes take effect immediately — there's no need to save manually for in-app and push toggles.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage email notifications separately">
    For email notifications, tap **Email Preferences** to open a dedicated screen where you can enable or disable each type of email alert individually.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Save">
    Once you've finished adjusting your email preferences, tap **Save** to confirm your choices and return to the main Notifications screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Device-Level Permissions

Push notifications in Uku also depend on your device's operating system granting permission to the app. If you've never received push notifications from Uku, or if they've suddenly stopped, your device may have blocked them.

<Info>
  **To re-enable push notifications on your device:**

  - **iOS:** Go to your phone's **Settings** → scroll down and tap **Uku** → tap **Notifications** → enable **Allow Notifications** and choose your preferred alert style.
  - **Android:** Go to your phone's **Settings** → **Apps** → tap **Uku** → tap **Notifications** → toggle **All Uku notifications** on.

  Once device-level permissions are restored, Uku's push notifications will resume automatically based on your in-app preferences.
</Info>

<Tip>
  Use **Do Not Disturb hours** to automatically silence Uku notifications during the night or other quiet periods. Go to **Settings → Notifications → Do Not Disturb**, then set a start and end time. Notifications will be held and delivered once the quiet window ends.
</Tip>
