Uku's settings let you tailor the app to your needs — from language and appearance to data and privacy. Every preference you adjust is saved to your account, so the app always feels like yours no matter which device you're on.

## Accessing Settings

To open Settings, tap your **profile avatar** in the top-right corner of any screen. In the menu that appears, select **Settings**. You'll land on the main settings panel, where all options are organized into clearly labeled sections.

## General Settings

The **General** section covers the everyday preferences that affect how Uku looks and behaves. Here's what you'll find:

- **Language** — Change the language Uku displays throughout the app. See [Changing Your Language](#changing-your-language) below for step-by-step instructions.
- **Theme** — Choose between **Light**, **Dark**, or **System default** to control the app's color scheme.
- **Units and Formats** — Set your preferred **date format** (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY or DD/MM/YYYY) and **time format** (12-hour or 24-hour clock) so information is always displayed the way you expect.

## Changing Your Language

Uku supports multiple display languages, and switching is instant. Here's how:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Tap your profile avatar (top-right) and select **Settings** from the dropdown menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap 'Language'">
    In the General section, tap the **Language** option to open the language selection screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your preferred language">
    Scroll through the list and tap the language you want. A checkmark will appear next to your selection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the app to reload">
    Uku reloads automatically and immediately displays everything in your chosen language. No restart needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Appearance

You can switch between **Light mode**, **Dark mode**, and **System default** at any time from **Settings → General → Theme**.

- **Light** — A bright, white-background interface suited for well-lit environments.
- **Dark** — A dark-background interface that's easier on the eyes in low-light conditions and can help conserve battery on OLED screens.
- **System default** — Uku automatically matches whichever theme your device OS is currently using. When your phone switches to dark mode at sunset, Uku follows along.

To change your theme, simply tap your current selection and choose a new one — the change takes effect instantly.

<Tip>
  Your settings sync across all your devices automatically whenever you're signed in to the same Uku account. Change a preference on your phone and it will be reflected on your tablet or web browser the next time you open the app.
</Tip>
