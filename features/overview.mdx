## What's New

Uku is always improving. New features, improvements, and fixes are rolled out regularly so the app keeps getting better over time. To see what's changed, open the in-app changelog by navigating to **Settings → About → What's New**. You can also follow the official release notes for a full history of updates and upcoming changes.

<Tip>
  Don't feel like you need to learn everything at once. Explore each section at your own pace — most features are intuitive enough to pick up as you go.
</Tip>
