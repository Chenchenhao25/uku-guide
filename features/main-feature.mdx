Uku's core features are designed to be simple to use while being powerful enough to support your daily workflow. Whether you're a first-time user or a seasoned pro, understanding these building blocks will help you get the most out of every session.

## Getting to the Features

From the home screen, you can reach the main features area in just a couple of taps. Look for the bottom navigation bar and tap the **Home** icon to return to your dashboard at any time, or use the menu icon (☰) in the top-left corner to open the full navigation panel. From there, every feature in Uku is one tap away.

## Using the Main Dashboard

The main dashboard is your central hub in Uku. At the top, you'll find **overview cards** that surface the information most relevant to you — things like recent activity, progress summaries, and at-a-glance stats. Below that, a row of **quick-action buttons** lets you jump straight into the most common tasks without digging through menus. Further down, the **Recent Activity** feed keeps a running log of everything you've done so you can pick up right where you left off. Take a moment to explore each section — hovering over or tapping any card or button will reveal more detail and additional options.

## Performing Key Actions

Creating something new in Uku takes just a few seconds. Here's how to do it:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap the '+' button">
    On the home screen, locate the **+** button in the bottom-right corner and tap it to begin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the type of item">
    A menu will appear listing the available item types. Select the one that fits what you want to create.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the required details">
    Complete the fields in the creation form. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (*). Optional fields let you add extra context or customization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save or confirm">
    When you're satisfied with the details, tap **Save** or **Confirm** to finish. Your new item will appear immediately in your dashboard and activity feed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips & Shortcuts

Once you're comfortable with the basics, these shortcuts can help you move faster and work smarter:

- **Long-press any item** in your dashboard to reveal a quick-action context menu with common options like edit, share, and delete.
- **Swipe left** on a recent activity entry to archive or remove it without opening the full detail view.
- **Use the search bar** (tap the magnifying glass icon) to instantly find any item, setting, or piece of content across the entire app.
- **Pin your most-used items** to the top of your dashboard by opening the item and tapping the pin (📌) icon in the top-right corner.
