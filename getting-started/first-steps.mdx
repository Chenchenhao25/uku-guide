Welcome to Uku! Now that your account is set up, it's time to get familiar with the app. This page gives you a quick orientation so you can find your way around with confidence, no matter which device you're using. You don't need to learn everything at once — just start here and explore from there.

## The Main Screen

When you open Uku, you'll land on your **Home** screen. The main navigation is always within easy reach, typically as a bar at the bottom of the screen on mobile or a sidebar on the web. Here's what each section gives you access to:

- 🏠 **Home** — your personal dashboard, showing an overview of your activity, highlights, and quick-access shortcuts.
- ✨ **Features** — the heart of the app, where you'll find all of Uku's core tools and capabilities organized for easy discovery.
- 👤 **Profile** — view and edit your personal information, profile photo, and account details.
- ⚙️ **Settings** — control your preferences, including language, notifications, privacy, and subscription options.

## Switch Your Language

Uku's multi-language support means you can use the app in the language you're most comfortable with. Switching is instant and applies across the entire app.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Tap the **Settings** icon in the main navigation (the gear icon ⚙️). This is found in the bottom navigation bar on mobile, or in the sidebar on the web.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Language">
    Scroll to the **Preferences** section and tap **Language**. You'll see the full list of languages currently supported by Uku.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Your Preferred Language">
    Tap the language you'd like to use. The app switches to your chosen language immediately — no restart or reload needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>
