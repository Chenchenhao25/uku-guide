Uku is available wherever you are. Install it on your iPhone or iPad, download it on your Android device, or open it instantly in any modern web browser — no installation required. Getting Uku up and running takes just a few moments, and you can pick up right where you left off across all your devices.

## How to Get Uku

Follow the instructions for your platform below:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iOS">
    1. Open the **App Store** on your iPhone or iPad.
    2. Tap the **Search** tab and type **"Uku"** in the search bar.
    3. Locate the official Uku app in the results and tap **Get**.
    4. Authenticate with Face ID, Touch ID, or your Apple ID password to confirm the download.
    5. Once installed, tap **Open** to launch Uku.

    **Minimum requirement:** iOS 14 or later.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    1. Open **Google Play** on your Android device.
    2. Tap the **Search** bar at the top and type **"Uku"**.
    3. Locate the official Uku app in the results and tap **Install**.
    4. Accept any permissions requested and wait for the download to complete.
    5. Tap **Open** to launch Uku.

    **Minimum requirement:** Android 8.0 (Oreo) or later.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Web">
    1. Open your preferred web browser on any computer or device.
    2. Navigate to **[app.uku.app](https://app.uku.app)**.
    3. The Uku web app loads instantly — no installation or plug-in needed.
    4. Bookmark the page for quick access next time.

    **Supported browsers:** Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge (latest two major versions of each).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## System Requirements

| Platform        | Minimum Version                          |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| iOS             | iOS 14 or later                          |
| Android         | Android 8.0 (Oreo) or later             |
| Web Browsers    | Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge (current and previous major release) |

<Note>
  Uku is free to download and install on all platforms. Some advanced features may require a paid subscription. You can explore available plans from within the app at any time.
</Note>
