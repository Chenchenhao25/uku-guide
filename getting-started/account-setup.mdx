Creating your Uku account is the first step to unlocking everything the app has to offer. The process takes just a few minutes, and you only need to do it once — after that, your account travels with you across every device you use. Follow the steps below to get set up and ready to go.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the App">
    Launch Uku on your device or navigate to [app.uku.app](https://app.uku.app) in your browser. On the welcome screen, tap or click **Get Started** or **Sign Up** to begin the registration process.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Your Sign-Up Method">
    Select how you'd like to create your account:

    - **Email & Password** — enter your email address and create a secure password.
    - **Continue with Google** — sign in using your existing Google account.
    - **Continue with Apple** — sign in using your Apple ID (iOS and web only).

    All methods are equally secure. Choose whichever is most convenient for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Your Email">
    If you signed up with an email address, Uku will send a verification email to your inbox. Open the email and click the **Verify My Email** link to confirm your address. If you don't see the email within a couple of minutes, check your spam or junk folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Your Profile">
    Once verified, you'll be guided through a short profile setup:

    - **Your name** — enter the name you'd like displayed in the app.
    - **Language preference** — choose the language you want Uku to use. You can change this any time in Settings.
    - **Profile photo** — upload a photo to personalize your account (optional, but recommended).
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're All Set!">
    That's it — your Uku account is ready. You'll be taken straight to your Home screen where you can start exploring all that Uku has to offer. Welcome aboard!
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  For your security, passwords must be **at least 8 characters long** and include **at least one number**. We also recommend using a mix of uppercase and lowercase letters for a stronger password.
</Note>

## Already Have an Account?

If you've used Uku before or previously created an account on another device, you don't need to sign up again. Simply tap **Log In** on the welcome screen and enter your credentials. To update your profile details after logging in, visit your [profile management page](/account/manage-profile).
