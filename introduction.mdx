Uku is a versatile, user-friendly app designed to help you organize your daily life, stay connected with what matters most, track your progress, and get things done — all from one place. Whether you're managing personal tasks, exploring features tailored to your lifestyle, or simply looking for a smarter way to stay on top of your day, Uku adapts to you. Best of all, multi-language support is built right in, so you can use Uku comfortably in the language you know best.

## Available Languages

Uku is built for a global audience. The app is currently available in:

- 🇬🇧 **English**
- 🇮🇹 **Italian**
- 🇫🇷 **French**
- 🇪🇸 **Spanish**
- 🇩🇪 **German**
- And more languages are being added regularly!

You can switch your language at any time directly from the app settings — no reinstall required, and your change takes effect immediately.
